## Hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. (or its parent company Microsoft Corporation). When
the site is called up, GitHub as technical service provider automatically processes server log data that your browser
transmits, in particular IP address, date and time of the request, the page called up and the browser type. This
processing serves to provide the website technically (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR) and is outside our direct influence. A
transfer of data to the USA cannot be ruled out; details are in
[GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Contact by e-mail

If you contact us by e-mail, the data you give (such as e-mail address, name, content of the message) are stored to
handle your request (Art. 6 (1) (b) or (f) GDPR). We do not pass these data on without your consent.

The data are deleted once your request has been fully handled, unless legal retention duties stand against it.

## Your rights

You have the right at any time to:

- erasure of your data (Art. 17 GDPR)
- restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
- data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)
- object to the processing (Art. 21 GDPR)

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority, such as the Berlin Commissioner for
Data Protection and Freedom of Information.
